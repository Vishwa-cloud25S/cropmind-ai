# 05 — UK Strategy (Innovator Founder route)

**Prepared:** 2026-09-28 · **Primary source:** [GOV.UK — Innovator Founder and Scale-up
visas: guidance for endorsing bodies](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/scale-up-and-innovator-founder-visa-endorsing-bodies-guidance/innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas-guidance-for-endorsing-bodies-accessible) (accessed 2026-09-28).

## 1. The route and its criteria (authoritative wording)

The endorsing body must assess a new business as **Innovative, Viable and Scalable**:

- **Innovation** — "a genuine, original business plan that meets new or existing market
  needs and/or creates a competitive advantage."
- **Viability** — "the business plan must be realistic and achievable based on the
  applicant's available resources"; the founder must have or be developing the skills,
  knowledge, experience and market awareness; financial projections "credibly defended";
  "credible demand."
- **Scalability** — "evidence of structured planning demonstrating a credible path to
  growth; potential for ongoing high quality and skilled job creation; scope for growth
  into national and international markets; projections based on credible research."

There is **no fixed minimum investment** in the Immigration Rules (the former £50k
requirement was abolished); maintenance funds of **£1,270 held 28 days** apply unless
exempt ([overview](https://overseaspacific.com/blog/uk-innovator-founder-visa-2025-2026-success-rate-requirements-fees-ilr-pathway),
[DavidsonMorris guide](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/innovator-founder-visa/) — both accessed 2026-09-28;
always confirm against current GOV.UK pages at application time).

**Endorsing bodies** are Home Office-approved; at the cited overview's publication the list
included Envestors, UK Endorsement Services (UKES), Innovator International, and the
invitation-only Global Entrepreneurs Programme — **the live list on GOV.UK governs**;
bodies change and must be re-verified at application.
